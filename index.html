1. Overview

Quintero Studios respects your privacy. This policy explains how OtraVida, our life simulation game, handles information. The game can be played offline, and optional in-app purchases require an internet connection.

2. Game information and local storage

OtraVida does not require you to create an account or submit personal details to play. Game progress, characters, virtual finances, achievements, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Quintero Studios does not operate a server that receives or stores your saved games.

3. In-app purchases

Optional digital purchases are handled through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments; Quintero Studios does not directly receive or store your complete payment-card details. The app may receive purchase status and transaction identifiers needed to unlock purchases and restore eligible purchases. Google's processing of payment and related information is subject to its own policies.

4. Internet access and third-party services

Normal gameplay works without internet access. Internet access is needed for purchases and purchase restoration. The app is distributed through Google Play and uses Google Play Billing for purchases. Google may process information as described in its Privacy Policy.

5. Data retention and deletion

Saved game data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings. Clearing data or uninstalling may permanently remove game progress. Google Play may retain purchase records under its own policies.

6. Security

Locally stored game data is subject to your device's security protections. We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

OtraVida does not require account registration or personal details to play. The game's intended audience and age rating are identified in its Google Play listing. Parents and guardians should review the game's content and available purchase controls.

8. Your choices and questions

You can manage locally stored game data through your device settings. For questions about this policy or the game's data practices, contact babylingoai@gmail.com. For information about Google-managed purchase data, see Google's privacy resources.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game, its services, or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised text on this page and update the effective date.
